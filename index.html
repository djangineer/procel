06 /WELDING ELECTRODES

PACE Weld electrodes

Procel Tech Services is the exclusive distributor of PACE Weld electrodes in Nigeria.

Made by an established Indian welding-electrode manufacturer certified to ISO 9001:2015, PACE offers consistent performance for industrial fabrication and maintenance.

  • Reliable weld quality and arc stability
  • Reduced spatter and efficient deposition
  • Dependable mechanical properties
Lower total welding cost—not just a lower purchase price. Better deposition efficiency, less electrode wastage, lower cleanup requirements and reduced rework can all help reduce overall cost.
